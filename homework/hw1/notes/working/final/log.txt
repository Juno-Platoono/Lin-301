==Regex Challenges==
1.  Every instance where a word begins with "Holmes" or "Watson."
    in>$ grep -Eo "\<(Watson|Holmes)" holmes.txt
    out>Holmes
        Holmes
        Holmes
        Holmes
        Holmes
        Holmes
        Holmes
        Watson
        Watson... etc.
2.  All five-letter words in the file.
    in>$ grep -Eo "\<([A-Z]|[a-z])([A-Z]|[a-z])([A-Z]|[a-z])([A-Z]|[a-z])([A-Z]|[a-z])\>" holmes.txt
    [i know how ugly that is, but does it not work?]
    out>shall
        train
        Yours
        which
        final
        whole
        would... etc.
3.  All lines that end in a question mark (remember `?` is a special character in regex — you'll need to escape it with `\`).
    in>$ grep -E "\?$" holmes.txt
    out>to do their own secreting. Why should she hand it over to anyone else?
        me. What was this nocturnal expedition, and why should I go armed?
        “It’s all clear,” he whispered. “Have you the chisel and the bags?
        mine. And yet, what could have happened? And why could he not write?...etc.
4.  All instances of a letter repeated twice in a row (like "ee" in "sleep" or "ss" in "glass"), using a backreference.
    in>$ grep -Eo "([a-z])\1" holmes.txt
    out>ll
        ee
        ll
        tt
        ll
        cc
        tt
        ll
        ss
        tt... etc
5.  The total number of times "he" or "He" or "she" or "She" appears as its own word (not buried inside a longer word like "the" or "shelf")
    in>$ grep -Ec "\<(he|He|she|She)\>" holmes.txt
    out>1697
